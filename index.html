Core transportation plan

Getting around

The arrival rail sequence, driving cautions, and day-trip packing list that apply across the whole Tuscany leg.

Arrival Rail Sequence

  • FCO → Roma Termini: Leonardo Express.
  • Roma Termini → Firenze Santa Maria Novella: reserved high-speed Frecciarossa or Italo train.
  • Firenze SMN → Florence Airport: T2 tram to the airport rental-car area.
  • Use separate contactless payment methods for the paying adults on Florence transit; verify current child-fare rules before travel.
  • Keep generous connection buffers after the overnight flight — the Monday afternoon plan is intentionally flexible.

Driving Cautions

  • Avoid city-center ZTL zones. Navigate directly to named garages and official parking areas.
  • Keep luggage hidden in the trunk before arriving at sightseeing parking lots.
  • Tuscan roads can be narrow and winding. Build more time than the navigation estimate, especially after dark.
  • One adult should remain a designated driver on wine-tour day.

Packing for Day Trips

  • Lightweight stroller or compact carrier
  • Refillable water bottles
  • Small snack kit
  • Sun hats and sunscreen
  • Portable charger
  • Photo ID for ticketed tours
  • A small layer for churches and air-conditioned trains
