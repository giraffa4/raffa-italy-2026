Core transportation plan

Getting around

The arrival rail sequence, driving cautions, and day-trip packing list that apply across the whole Tuscany leg.

Arrival Rail Sequence

  • FCO → Roma Termini: Leonardo Express.
  • Roma Termini → Firenze Santa Maria Novella: reserved high-speed Frecciarossa or Italo train.
  • Firenze SMN → Florence Airport: T2 tram to the airport rental-car area.
  • Use separate contactless payment methods for the paying adults on Florence transit; verify current child-fare rules before travel.
  • Keep generous connection buffers after the overnight flight — the Monday afternoon plan is intentionally flexible.

Driving Cautions

  • Avoid city-center ZTL zones. Navigate directly to named garages and official parking areas.
  • Keep luggage hidden in the trunk before arriving at sightseeing parking lots.
  • Tuscan roads can be narrow and winding. Build more time than the navigation estimate, especially after dark.
  • One adult should remain a designated driver on wine-tour day.

Packing for Day Trips

  • Lightweight stroller or compact carrier
  • Refillable water bottles
  • Small snack kit
  • Sun hats and sunscreen
  • Portable charger
  • Photo ID for ticketed tours
  • A small layer for churches and air-conditioned trains

Delay Rule
  • If the rental car is delayed past about 3:00 PM, shorten or skip the Impruneta stroll. Prioritize groceries, Casa di Miei check-in, and the 7:30 PM Bellavista reservation.
Impruneta Arrival Ideas & Parking
  • Piazza Buondelmonti: the main square, an easy first stop and introduction to the town.
  • Basilica di Santa Maria all'Impruneta: step inside if open, or simply enjoy the piazza and facade.
  • Terracotta browsing: look for a local shop; Fornace Agresti is an optional bonus only if timing and opening hours cooperate.
  • Use signed public parking near Piazza Buondelmonti and follow local restrictions — there's no need to drive into Florence on Monday.
  • Keep the stop compact and low-pressure so a flight or rail delay doesn't derail the evening. If everyone is tired, skip sightseeing and go directly to groceries and Casa di Miei.
  • Coop Impruneta is most convenient for the Airbnb and a complete first-night shop. Coop Tavarnuzze is a larger backup if it falls naturally on the route. Forno Zini is an optional bakery add-on for bread and breakfast pastries.
Dinner: Ristoro Bellavista
  • Best reservation time: 7:30 PM. Located beside Piazza Buondelmonti, so dinner keeps the first evening centered on Impruneta.
  • Family-friendly with straightforward pasta, chicken, potatoes and other child-friendly choices.
  • If the family is fading, ask whether takeaway is available or simplify dinner at the Airbnb.
Raffa Tip
  • Monday is about arriving well, not sightseeing hard. A short walk through Impruneta, groceries, time at Casa di Miei, and Bellavista dinner is a successful first day.

Harvest Question
  • September may overlap with vendemmia. Ask Palagina whether the family can observe grape picking, sorting or early winemaking activity during the visit.
Montefioralle & Greve Parking Guide
  • Montefioralle: navigate to signed visitor parking outside the historic village — do not drive through the medieval center. Primary: Piazza Santo Stefano when available. Backup: the signed gravel visitor lot below or outside the village approach. Expect uneven stone paving and slopes; a carrier may be easier than a large stroller.
  • Greve in Chianti: navigate to a lot outside the historic-center ZTL, not directly into Piazza Matteotti. Options: Piazza Resistenza, Piazzale Ferrante Mori, or Piazzale Finetti. Pay at the meter or approved app and keep the receipt visible. ZTL hours can change seasonally — follow current signs.
  • In Greve: walk the arcades around Piazza Matteotti, browse Antica Macelleria Falorni for salumi and cheese, and finish with gelato or coffee rather than another formal tour.
Dinner: Hostaria del Bricco in San Niccolò
  • Best reservation time: 7:30 PM. Leave Greve around 5:00 PM, reset at Casa di Miei, then depart for Florence around 6:15 PM.
  • Primary parking: paid spaces around Piazzale Michelangelo. Backup: Parcheggio Beccaria if the piazzale is full.
  • Enjoy the view before dinner, then walk downhill to Via di San Niccolò. The return walk to Piazzale Michelangelo is uphill.
Raffa Tip
  • Tuesday now has two acts: Chianti countryside by day, then a Florence sunset and San Niccolò dinner. Keep Greve departure close to 5:00 PM so the evening never feels rushed.

ZTL Rule
  • Set navigation directly to Parcheggio Ex-Staveco. Do not accept last-minute shortcuts through the historic center, where camera-enforced restrictions operate.
If Timing Slips — Priority Order
  • Protect the Quadrilatero, handmade pasta, Piazza Maggiore, Santo Stefano, and the two recharge stops (Salaborsa and Giardini Margherita).
  • Via Piella canal window and the Archiginnasio Anatomical Theatre are the easiest optional cuts.
Bologna Must-Eat Shortlist
  • Tagliatelle al ragù · Tortellini in brodo · Tortelloni
  • Mortadella · Parmigiano-Reggiano
  • Crescentine or tigelle, when available
  • Traditional balsamic vinegar tasting · a local dessert such as zuppa inglese · artisan gelato
Rain Plan & Dinner Backups
  • Bologna's porticoes make bad weather manageable — extend Salaborsa, browse the Quadrilatero slowly, and shorten Giardini Margherita rather than abandoning the day.
  • Dinner backups if Osteria Al 15 isn't available: Trattoria Amedeo, Trattoria Anna Maria (reserve well ahead), or Trattoria da Me.
