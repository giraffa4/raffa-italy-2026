Palagina & the Tuscan Countryside

Breakfast at the Airbnb

Depart for Palagina

Confirm the exact meeting point and drive time in advance.

Palagina wine tour

Family-friendly winery experience centered on the vineyard, wine, olive oil, and the estate. Confirm the final duration and whether lunch is included.

After tour

Lunch at or near Palagina

Keep the meal unhurried and let the children reset before any afternoon activity.

Afternoon

Flexible countryside activity

Choose Impruneta, a short terracotta experience, Greve, Montefioralle, or simply enjoy the property depending on energy and weather.

5:30 PM

Scenic aperitivo or gelato stop

Finish in a village square or at a scenic viewpoint rather than adding another formal attraction.

6:30–7:00 PM

Return to Impruneta

Dinner near the Airbnb or cook at the villa.

Best Afternoon Choices
  • Impruneta village: Piazza Buondelmonti, the Basilica, cafés, gelato, and an easy local walk.
  • Terracotta experience: visit a local workshop or showroom to see the craft Impruneta is famous for.
  • Greve in Chianti: Piazza Matteotti, local food shops, cafés, and a relaxed village atmosphere.
  • Montefioralle: a short walk through the small medieval hill village overlooking Greve.
  • Scenic countryside drive: back roads through vineyards and olive groves, with viewpoints and small villages.
Raffa Notes
  • Keep the afternoon flexible — Palagina plus one or two countryside stops will feel more enjoyable than trying to complete every option.

Built-in flexibility

The itinerary intentionally leaves room for weather, the children's energy levels, travel delays, and spontaneous discoveries. Monday afternoon remains intentionally open after the overnight flight and rail transfer. Tuesday is anchored by the 10:00 AM Palagina tour. Thursday is centered on the Accademia and the approximately 5:00 PM train to Rome. Friday and Saturday use the central Rome lodging as the walking base, with taxis available to preserve energy before major tours.

The Raffa rules of the road

Five things to remember mid-trip

One excellent experience is better than three rushed ones.

Gelato is a legitimate scheduling tool.

Use taxis before everyone is exhausted, not after.

Keep one optional stop every day that can be dropped without regret.

The best family memories may happen between the landmarks.
