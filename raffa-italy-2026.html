01

Arrival, Florence & Impruneta

Monday, September 7 Base: Impruneta

A deliberately easy landing day after the overnight flight.

9:00 AM

Arrive at Rome Fiumicino Airport

Allow roughly 90 minutes for immigration, baggage claim and the walk to the airport train station.

11:00 AM

Travel to Florence

Take the airport connection into Rome, then continue by high-speed train to Firenze Santa Maria Novella. Use your confirmed train plan.

1:30 PM

Pick up rental cars

Choose a pickup location that avoids driving deep into Florence's restricted ZTL whenever possible. Load luggage before beginning the city walk.

2:00 PM

Casual lunch at Mercato Centrale

An easy first meal where everyone can choose pasta, pizza, sandwiches or other local specialties without committing to a long formal lunch.

3:15 PM

Easy Florence highlights walk

Follow a relaxed loop through Piazza del Duomo, Piazza della Repubblica, Piazza della Signoria and Ponte Vecchio. Skip museums today.

4:30 PM

Gelato and reset

Stop at Gelateria dei Neri, Perché No! or La Carraia. Sit down, hydrate and decide whether the kids have energy for another short stroll.

5:30 PM

Drive to Impruneta

Stop at a Coop or Conad for breakfast food, water, snacks and anything needed for the Airbnb.

7:00 PM

Easy dinner and early bedtime

Stay close to the Airbnb and keep dinner simple. The goal is to reset for Tuesday.

Raffa Notes
  • Do not plan a timed attraction on arrival day.
  • Expect everyone's energy to drop sharply in the late afternoon.
  • Keep pajamas and next-day clothes easy to reach in the luggage.
02

Bologna Food Adventure

Tuesday, September 8 Base: Impruneta

A full day built around regional specialties, markets and easy sightseeing.

7:00 AM

Breakfast at the Airbnb

Quick breakfast; bring water plus a few kid snacks for the drive.

8:00 AM

Leave Impruneta for Bologna

Allow approximately 1 hour 30 minutes, plus parking time. A central garage outside restricted zones is the simplest option.

9:45 AM

Coffee and pastry

Start at Caffè Terzi or another traditional bar near the center.

10:15 AM

Explore the Quadrilatero

Browse the historic food district and sample Parmigiano Reggiano, mortadella, prosciutto and balsamic vinegar.

11:30 AM

Fresh pasta lunch

Try Sfoglia Rina, Tamburini or Salumeria Simoni. Order several dishes to share — tagliatelle al ragù, tortellini and tortelloni.

1:00 PM

Mercato delle Erbe

Continue the food experience at the market, with time to browse local produce, cheese and specialty shops.

2:00 PM

Gelato stop

Cremeria Santo Stefano is a strong destination; another excellent gelateria nearby works just as well if the route changes.

2:30 PM

Piazza Maggiore and easy sightseeing

Walk Piazza Maggiore, see the Basilica of San Petronio and explore the surrounding porticoes and side streets.

3:45 PM

Kid-friendly optional activity

Choose the San Luca Express tourist train, or continue wandering and shopping. Skip the tower climb unless everyone is enthusiastic.

5:00 PM

Return to Impruneta

Head back before the evening traffic builds.

7:00 PM

Dinner near the Airbnb

Keep dinner casual after the food-heavy day.

Raffa Notes
  • Bologna is best treated as a grazing day — share dishes rather than ordering a full meal at every stop.
  • Porticoes make this day workable even in light rain or strong sun.
03

Tuscan Countryside Day

Wednesday, September 9 Base: Impruneta

A slower family day with farm experiences, wine, villages and open space.

8:30 AM

Slow breakfast

Sleep in, enjoy the Airbnb and give the kids an unhurried start.

10:00 AM

Leave for the countryside experience

Drive toward a family-friendly estate. Fattoria di Maiano is the best fit if farm animals and outdoor space are priorities.

10:30 AM

Farm, olive oil and wine experience

Adults can enjoy a tasting while the children explore the farm setting, animals and walking areas. Confirm the family experience and tasting time directly with the estate.

12:30 PM

Lunch at or near the estate

Choose the estate restaurant if available, or a nearby trattoria with outdoor seating.

2:30 PM

Explore Greve in Chianti

Walk the main piazza, browse local food and wine shops, and take an afternoon coffee or gelato break.

4:00 PM

Optional Montefioralle stop

Visit the small medieval hilltop village above Greve. Keep this optional so the day still feels relaxed.

5:30 PM

Return to Impruneta

Enjoy downtime at the Airbnb before dinner.

7:00 PM

Dinner in or near Impruneta

Cook at the Airbnb or choose a casual local trattoria. Peposo, the area's slow-cooked peppery beef specialty, is worth trying.

Raffa Notes
  • Reserve the winery or farm experience in advance and confirm that children are welcome.
  • Avoid stacking multiple wineries — one quality experience is enough with kids.
04

Florence Highlights & Train to Rome

Thursday, September 10 Base: Rome (from tonight)

A focused sightseeing day with luggage logistics and a firm departure time.

7:30 AM

Breakfast, pack and leave Impruneta

Build in extra time for final packing and the drive into Florence.

9:00 AM

Return rental cars and store luggage

Return the cars, then leave luggage at your hotel, the station, or a confirmed storage facility.

10:00 AM

Accademia Gallery

Book a timed entry and allow about 75–90 minutes to see Michelangelo's David and the gallery highlights.

11:30 AM

Duomo district

Walk around the cathedral, Baptistery and Giotto's Bell Tower. Only add a climb if tickets are reserved and the family wants the stairs.

1:00 PM

Lunch

Choose Mercato Centrale for flexibility or Trattoria ZaZa for a seated Tuscan meal.

2:15 PM

Ponte Vecchio and Oltrarno

Cross the river, browse artisan workshops and enjoy a relaxed final walk through Florence.

3:30 PM

Piazzale Michelangelo

Take a taxi for the panoramic city view, then return directly toward the station.

4:20 PM

Collect luggage and board

Be at Santa Maria Novella with enough time to retrieve luggage, find the platform and settle in.

5:00 PM

Train to Rome

Travel to Rome, check into your accommodations and have dinner nearby.

Raffa Notes
  • Treat 3:45 PM as the hard cutoff for sightseeing.
  • A taxi to and from Piazzale Michelangelo protects time and energy.
05

Ancient Rome Marathon

Friday, September 11 Base: Rome

The most ambitious day of the trip — major archaeological sites followed by the historic center.

7:30 AM

Breakfast and depart

Eat early and arrive near the Colosseum before your timed entry.

8:30 AM

Colosseum

Book one of the earliest available entries. A family-oriented guide can make the history much more engaging for children.

10:15 AM

Roman Forum

Continue into the Forum while temperatures and crowds are still manageable.

11:45 AM

Palatine Hill

Finish the archaeological complex and enjoy the elevated views.

1:15 PM

Lunch in Monti

Sit down for pizza, pasta or Roman classics. Ai Tre Scalini is a good neighborhood option; reserve elsewhere for a guaranteed table.

2:45 PM

Capitoline Hill and Piazza Venezia

See the Capitoline area and the Victor Emmanuel II Monument. Use the elevator viewpoint only if timing and energy allow.

4:00 PM

Trevi Fountain

Make the traditional coin toss, take photos and keep the stop relatively short.

4:45 PM

Pantheon

Book timed entry if required and allow about 30–45 minutes.

5:45 PM

Piazza Navona

Slow down, enjoy the fountains and give the kids room to recharge.

7:15 PM

Dinner in Trastevere

Take a taxi if needed. Reserve dinner so the family is not waiting after a long day.

9:00 PM

Optional evening walk

Stroll a short section of the Tiber, or return to the accommodations for an earlier night.

Raffa Notes
  • Wear the best walking shoes you own.
  • Carry refillable bottles and use Rome's public drinking fountains.
  • A taxi between Piazza Navona and Trastevere may be worthwhile by evening.
06

Vatican City & Final Rome Evening

Saturday, September 12 Base: Rome

A second packed day anchored by the Vatican, followed by food-focused neighborhoods.

7:15 AM

Breakfast and depart

Arrive early enough to clear security before the first major crowds.

8:00 AM

Vatican Museums

Reserve the earliest practical entry. Focus on the major galleries and the Sistine Chapel rather than trying to see every room.

11:00 AM

St. Peter's Basilica

Walk through the basilica and allow time for security. Dress codes apply.

12:15 PM

Optional dome climb

Choose this only if the kids still have energy for stairs and enclosed spaces. Otherwise enjoy St. Peter's Square.

1:30 PM

Lunch near the Vatican

Pick a well-reviewed restaurant a few blocks away from the main tourist corridor.

3:00 PM

Castel Sant'Angelo

Tour the fortress or simply enjoy the exterior and riverfront depending on energy.

4:30 PM

Ponte Sant'Angelo and Campo de' Fiori

Cross the bridge and continue through the historic streets toward the market square.

6:00 PM

Jewish Ghetto

Explore the neighborhood and try Roman-Jewish specialties. Fried artichokes are seasonal.

7:30 PM

Final dinner

Reserve a memorable final meal near the Jewish Ghetto, Campo de' Fiori or your accommodations.

Raffa Notes
  • The Vatican is easier with a guide who can keep children engaged and navigate the museum efficiently.
  • Should the family be exhausted, skip the dome and tour Castel Sant'Angelo only from the outside.

Daily family essentials

The Raffa rules of the road

Five things to remember mid-trip

One excellent experience is better than three rushed ones.

Gelato is a legitimate scheduling tool.

Use taxis before everyone is exhausted, not after.

Keep one optional stop every day that can be dropped without regret.

The best family memories may happen between the landmarks.
